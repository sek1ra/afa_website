Hackey Bounce

This policy explains how Hackey Bounce handles user data.

Effective date: September 10, 2026

App: Hackey Bounce

Developer: Apex Flex Apps

Introduction

Hackey Bounce is a simple game app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how information is used, and how to contact Apex Flex Apps with privacy questions.

Information We Collect

Hackey Bounce does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not require account registration, login, email address, name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or payment information.

Internet Access

Hackey Bounce does not require an internet connection to function.

The app is designed to run locally on the device and does not send gameplay information to Apex Flex Apps servers.

Advertising and Analytics

Hackey Bounce does not include advertising, ad networks, analytics tools, tracking pixels, or third-party tracking services.

Data Sharing

Because Hackey Bounce does not collect personal information, Apex Flex Apps does not sell, share, or disclose personal information from the app to third parties.

Local Data

The app may store basic game state or settings locally on your device, if needed for normal gameplay. Local data stays on your device and is not transmitted to Apex Flex Apps.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Hackey Bounce does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Apex Flex Apps may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Apex Flex Apps at support@apexflexapps.com.
