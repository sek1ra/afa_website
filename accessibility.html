Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Apex Flex Apps is committed to making its website and apps accessible and usable for as many people as possible.

Our Approach

We aim to provide clear navigation, readable text, keyboard-friendly interactions, accessible contrast, and responsive layouts across the Apex Flex Apps website.

Feedback and Support

If you experience an accessibility issue with this website or with Chess Mate Puzzles, contact us at support@apexflexapps.com.

Please include the page or app feature involved, your device/browser or Android version, and a short description of the issue.
